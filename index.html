Privacy Policy for QScan

Last updated: December 2025

1. Overview

QScan is a simple QR code and barcode scanner application. The application works entirely locally on the user’s device and does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or non-personal user data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

QScan does not collect any of the following information:

  • Personal information (name, email address, phone number)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, advertising ID)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavior tracking data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs
  • Images, photos, or camera frames
  • Scanned QR codes or barcode contents

All scanning and decoding operations are performed in real time on the device and are not saved or transmitted.

3. Camera Permission

QScan requires access to the device camera solely for scanning QR codes and barcodes.

  • Camera access is used only while scanning
  • No images or video are recorded or stored
  • No camera data is transmitted from the device

4. Links and Scanned Content

If a scanned QR code or barcode contains a URL or other actionable information, it is displayed to the user. Any further action, such as opening a website, is performed only at the user’s request.

QScan does not control or monitor third-party websites and is not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

5. Third-Party Services

QScan does not use:

  • Third-party analytics services
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Cloud processing or storage services
  • Any form of data sharing with third parties

6. Data Storage

QScan does not store any user data locally or remotely. All functionality is provided without creating or maintaining user records.

7. Children’s Privacy

QScan does not knowingly collect any data from users of any age, including children under 13. Since no data is collected, the application complies with applicable children’s privacy regulations.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application’s functionality changes in a way that affects privacy. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
